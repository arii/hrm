# HRM System: End-to-End Workflow

This guide walks you through connecting your device, synchronizing music, and monitoring performance zones.

## 1. The Dashboard
Your central hub for monitoring real-time metrics.
![Dashboard Initial State](/assets/tutorial/01-dashboard-initial.png)

## 2. Connecting a Device
Navigate to the **Connect** tab. Here you can pair standard Bluetooth LE Heart Rate Monitors (Polar, Garmin) or use the Mock HRM for testing.
![Connect Page](/assets/tutorial/02-connect-page.png)

### Successful Connection
Once paired, the status indicator will turn green.
![Device Connected](/assets/tutorial/03-device-connected.png)

## 3. Spotify Integration
To sync music tempo with your workout intensity, log in to Spotify via the Control Panel.
![Spotify Login](/assets/tutorial/04-spotify-login-prompt.png)

## 4. Using the Timer
Configure your Tabata or HIIT intervals. The timer synchronizes across all connected clients via WebSockets.
![Timer Running](/assets/tutorial/05-timer-running.png)

## 5. Live Zone Monitoring
As your heart rate increases, the dashboard tiles update in real-time to reflect your current zone (Blue -> Green -> Orange -> Red).
![High Intensity Zone](/assets/tutorial/06-zone-high-intensity.png)
