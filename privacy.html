Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

1. Introduction

The HRM Dashboard ("we", "us") is designed to prioritize your privacy. We process your health data locally on your device.

2. Data Collection & Usage

  • Heart Rate Data: Streamed via Bluetooth for real-time visualization. This data is processed in your browser's memory and is not stored on our servers.
  • Strava Profile: If you connect with Strava, we access your public profile and heart rate configuration to personalize your zones.

We do not sell, license, or share your data with third parties or advertisers.

3. Strava Platform Disclosure

The following disclosure is required by the Strava API Agreement:

Strava may monitor and collect certain usage data and information related to your use of the Strava API Materials, and Strava Platform in connection with this Application, and Strava may use such Usage Data for any business purpose, internal or external, including, without limitation, providing enhancements to the Strava API Materials or Strava Platform, providing developer or user support, or ensuring compliance.

4. Data Retention

We practice data minimization. Health data is discarded immediately after your session ends unless you explicitly choose to upload it to Strava.
