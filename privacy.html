Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal heart rate data on our servers. Heart rate data is streamed live for visualization during your workout and is not saved.

When you connect your Strava account, we may access your Strava profile information in order to sync your workout data. This information is used solely for the purpose of integrating with the Strava API and is handled in accordance with Strava's API agreement.

How We Use Your Information

The information accessed from your Strava account is used to sync your workout data, including heart rate, to your private Strava account. We do not use this information for any other purpose.

Data Security

We are committed to ensuring that your information is secure. We use HTTPS encryption to protect data transmitted between your device and our servers.
