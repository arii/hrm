Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 5, 2025

HRM Monitor ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application and integrate with third-party services like Strava and Spotify.

1. Data Collection & Usage

We believe in Data Minimization. We only process data necessary for the functionality of the app:

  • Health Data: We read real-time Heart Rate (HR) data from your connected Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices to visualize your workout zones.
  • Strava Profile: We access your Strava profile solely to display your avatar and name on your personal dashboard.
  • Spotify Activity: We access your playback state (current track) to visualize the beat and control tempo.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your health data.

All heart rate and workout data is processed in real-time on your device or our ephemeral server session and is discarded immediately when you close the application or end the session.

Account Disconnection

You may disconnect your Strava or Spotify accounts at any time via the "Settings" menu in the app. Upon disconnection, all authentication tokens are immediately permanently deleted from our system. You may also revoke access directly via the Strava Apps Settings or Spotify Apps Settings pages.

3. Third-Party Services & Attribution

Our service integrates with the following platforms. Your use of these integrations is subject to their respective policies:

Strava & Garmin Data

We use the Strava API to sync and display activity data. In compliance with Strava API Brand Guidelines:

  • We do not use Strava data for any purpose other than providing the HRM Monitor functionality to you.
  • Garmin Attribution: Some activity data displayed in this application may originate from Garmin® devices. We acknowledge that this data is sourced from Garmin via Strava. Garmin is a trademark of Garmin Ltd. or its subsidiaries.

Spotify

Music metadata (Album Art, Track Name) is displayed using the Spotify Web API. We do not manipulate, store, or redistribute this content. Use of Spotify features is subject to the Spotify Privacy Policy.

4. AI/ML Prohibitions

We explicitly guarantee that no user data (including Heart Rate data, Strava activity history, or Spotify listening habits) is used to train Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Machine Learning (ML) models, either by us or third parties.
