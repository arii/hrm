Professional Grade Metrics

❤️ Universal HRM Connectivity

Built on the Web Bluetooth API to support the standard GATT Heart Rate Profile. Connects directly to any compliant hardware—including monitors from Polar, Garmin, and Wahoo—without proprietary hubs or native drivers.

🎵 Auto-Sync Spotify

Integrated via the Spotify Web Playback SDK. Playback state is tightly coupled to your workout timer: music automatically starts when you begin training and pauses when you stop, removing the need for manual DJing.
