How It Works

1

Open Connection Hub

Open the app in a Chrome-based browser. Enter your name and age to calculate your Max HR zones automatically.

2

Pair Device

Click "Connect Bluetooth HRM". Select your Heart Rate Monitor from the browser's pairing menu. Status will turn green.

3

Start a Workout

Use the built-in timer to start a workout. The timer will keep you on track with audio and visual cues.

4

Authorize Spotify

Authorize with Spotify (Premium account required) to play music directly from the dashboard. Playback is synchronized with the workout timer.

5

Phone Controller

Use your phone as a remote control for the timer and Spotify playback. Perfect for managing your workout without interrupting your flow.
